Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. Across Fountainebleau, Fountainebleau Restoration answers sewage emergencies 24/7, sending trained technicians who arrive with EPA-registered disinfectants and the right protective equipment so your property is decontaminated safely.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. Because of it, cleanup follows the toughest rules in the trade, which means full protective gear, barriers to contain the area, tearing out any porous material that met sewage and disinfecting all that is left. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

The cleanup process involves removing all standing sewage, extracting contaminated water from structural materials, removing and properly disposing of every porous material that contacted sewage (carpet, padding, drywall, insulation), thoroughly disinfecting hard surfaces, applying antimicrobial treatments and completing structural drying to prevent secondary mold growth. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Use drain strainers in showers and sinks to catch hair and debris
  • Schedule a camera inspection of the sewer line every 2 to 3 years so trouble is found early
  • Keep downspouts and sump pump discharge out of the sanitary sewer so it does not overload in heavy rain
  • Food waste, oils and grease block pipes, so put them in the trash and not the sink
  • Have roots removed where trees grow by the sewer, before they get inside the pipe and plug it
  • A backwater valve at the sewer connection stops the city system from backing up into your home

Signs You Need Sewage Cleanup in Fountainebleau

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Sluggish drainage at all fixtures, a sign the main line is blocking up
  • Foul-smelling moisture showing up on flooring or along the walls
  • Family members falling ill, especially with gastrointestinal trouble, for no obvious reason
  • Dark residue from sewage left on the basement floor or ringing a drain
  • Multiple drains in your home backing up at the same time
  • Wastewater seeping from the sewer cleanout or pooling in the yard above the line
  • Carpet or flooring contaminated when a toilet overflowed with solid waste

What a Professional Brings to Sewage Cleanup

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.
